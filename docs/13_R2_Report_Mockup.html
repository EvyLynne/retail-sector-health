Findings spec 4.9

Static text box

Over 114 months, aggregate case volume shows a slope of −0.41 against the stress composite with a correlation of −0.21 — weak, and broadly consistent with the inelastic-demand claim. But the category aggregate hides the channel: kegs run −2.41 while bottled liquor is near zero. The finding is that the on-premise channel absorbed the stress and the category did not. These are ordinary least squares fits over a short monthly series with no standard errors, no lag structure and no controls — direction and rough magnitude only, not a demand elasticity.
